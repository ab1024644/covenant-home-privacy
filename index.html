Covenant Home — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Covenant Home ("the app") is a faith-centered family app that helps households grow spiritually, manage money biblically, and encourage one another. This policy explains what information the app stores, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

When you use Covenant Home, the following information is stored so the app can work:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and an encrypted password.
  • Family information — your family's name, its members, their roles (parent, spouse, child, guardian, guest), and invite codes or invited email addresses.
  • Spiritual activity — which devotions, Bible readings, and prayer times you mark complete, and your streaks.
  • Financial entries you type in — income, expenses, giving, budgets, and savings goals that you or your family members log. The app never connects to your bank and never sees real account numbers or card details.
  • Family life entries — chores, calendar events, and encouragement messages sent between family members.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How your information is used

Your information is used for exactly one purpose: making the app work for you and your family. Data is shared within your family only — that sharing is the point of the app. It is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored with Supabase, a cloud database provider, protected by industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access is enforced by database-level security rules ("row-level security"), which means one family can never read another family's data. Financial visibility inside a family is role-based: members with the Child or Guest role do not see the family's financial information in the app.

The developer has administrative access to the database for maintenance and support purposes only, and does not review the contents of your family's data except where strictly necessary to resolve a support issue you raise.

Children

Covenant Home is designed for families, and children may use it as members of a family created and supervised by their parent or guardian. Children can only join a family through a parent's invite code or email invitation. We do not knowingly allow children to create independent accounts outside a family, and we collect from children only the same minimal information described above (name, email, and their activity within the family). Parents can remove a child from the family, or delete a child's account entirely, at any time.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists, so your family's history (streaks, budgets, reading progress) keeps working. When your account is deleted, your data is deleted with it.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: Family tab → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all data tied to it — devotion history, financial entries, chores, messages, and everything else. If you own a family that has other members, ownership passes to the longest-standing member and their data remains; if you are the only member, the family's data is deleted entirely. This cannot be undone.

You may also email us at the address below to request deletion.
